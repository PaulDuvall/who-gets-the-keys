# Owner queue, 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-11: lessons

The owner worked through steps 8 to 12 of `docs/notes/autonomous-run-2026-11-08.md`, then `proposals/alarm-key/` and the node-forge fix, with an agent in a cloud session giving one step at a time. Every batch landed, and every check and boundary held. The steps themselves were fragile: the owner stopped 13 times on something that was not the change under review. On 2026-11-10 the owner asked that these be prevented, not only fixed in place. Each row below says what happened, what it cost, and the backlog item that would prevent it.

Status: proposed. Nothing in "Prevent with" is built yet.
